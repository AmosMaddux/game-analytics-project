# Source rights: private technical pilot

Reviewed 10 October 2026. This is a concise reading of the project’s source-use review, not a legal opinion or provider approval.

## Approved scope

The user confirmed non-commercial use and a bounded technical-feasibility milestone. The private 36-game pilot can be assessed on that basis. This is not approval for a public, commercial, continuously refreshed, exportable or AI-enriched research product. Keeping a port private does not itself establish source rights.

## What remains gated

- **IGDB catalog:** the documented non-commercial API and local-cache path supports this bounded pilot. Applicable enduring research, retention and termination terms remain unresolved. Future commercial purpose requires review through IGDB’s partnership route; privacy or zero app revenue alone does not establish eligibility.
- **Steam APIs:** documented public methods support bounded validation under their terms. Preserve attribution, units, collection times and source separation. API availability does not confer blanket dataset-resale or bulk-export rights.
- **Steam tags:** the saved tags are dated lineage/method evidence. Automated store/tag collection and recurring refresh remain deferred until a permitted route is established. IGDB themes must not silently replace Steam tags.
- **Redistribution and assets:** public release, raw exports, cover art/screenshots, historical/derived commercial products and other downstream uses remain outside this approval.
- **AI processing:** no OpenAI data pipeline is enabled. A later design must establish source rights, minimal fields, processing purpose, retention and deletion behavior before transmitting data.

## Important uncertainty

IGDB expressly documents local caching. Twitch’s broader agreement may impose research/termination conditions, but the original review could not retrieve the full current agreement body from its canonical URL. An indexed official older body was only corroboration. The project therefore neither invents a universal IGDB 24-hour purge nor claims perpetual ownership or unconditional production rights.
